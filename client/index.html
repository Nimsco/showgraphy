Showgraphy · A personal watch journal

Your life,
in titles.

Showgraphy is a personal archive for the films and shows you watch. Mark titles as planned, watching, completed, on hold, or dropped. Keep episode progress, notes, dates, and personal ratings beside every entry. Make private watchlists and custom collections, then create a share link when you choose. Explore titles through configured catalog providers or add a missing one yourself. Your profile controls which statistics, collections, ratings, recent activity, and favorite titles other viewers can see. Keep your viewing history together and share only what you choose.
